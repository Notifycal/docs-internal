# Internationalization in Our App

## Introduction

Internationalization (i18n) is an essential aspect of building scalable applications that can adapt to users from different regions, languages, and cultures. In our app, we follow a set of principles that ensure we build an experience that is accessible and adaptable to a wide range of users, while adhering to global standards.

## Key Principles

### 1. Adopting Standards

Our approach to internationalization focuses on adopting industry standards wherever possible. This includes:

- **Languages:** We follow the [**ISO 639-1**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_639-1) standard for language codes, which provides two-letter codes for the representation of names of languages. Currently, we have implemented support for:
  - **es** (Spanish)
  - **en** (English)

  Although we use these two-letter codes, they represent specific locales of **es-ES** (Spanish - Spain) and **en-UK** (English - United Kingdom). This ensures that our application is adaptable to these two widely spoken languages while following the ISO 639-1 standard for language representation.

- **Countries:** We adhere to the [**ISO 3166-1**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_3166-1) standard for country codes, using:
  - **ES** for Spain
  - **GB** for the United Kingdom

  These two-letter country codes are used in conjunction with language codes to provide precise localization and regional specificity.

- **Phone Numbers:** We align our implementation with global standards for phone number formats, specifically the [**E.164**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E.164) standard, ensuring consistent validation and formatting across different countries. We use country codes and dial codes according to the **ITU-T E.164** recommendation, ensuring our app works reliably for users in different regions.

- **Currency (Future Implementation):** Although not implemented yet, we are preparing for future support of multiple currencies, following global standards for currency formatting and conversion. More once we integrate with a payment provider.

- **Time Zones:** We adopt the [**ISO 8601**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_8601) standard for representing date and time information. All calculations and data processing are done in **UTC** to ensure consistency and accuracy across different regions. The application only applies the user's **time zone** when presenting information, ensuring that, for example, **reminders** are shown in the correct **local time** for the user.

### 2. Visual and Conceptual Aids

While adhering to international standards, we also recognize the importance of providing a clear, intuitive user experience. To help with this, we incorporate the following visual aids:

- **Flags for Languages and Countries:** We use country flags as visual cues to help users identify language preferences and phone number formats easily. Although the flag represents a country, it is used primarily as a visual aid to indicate the associated language or phone number format.

  For example:
  - **🇪🇸** for Spanish (es-ES)
  - **🇬🇧** for English (en-UK)

  This helps users quickly navigate the app's language and phone number settings without confusion.

### 3. Progressive Implementation

While our internationalization efforts are ongoing, we prioritize adopting standards in a way that allows us to grow and scale the app. We follow a **progressive implementation** approach, meaning we continuously expand language, country, and phone number support as the app evolves. One important aspect that determines our adoption pace and scope is the messaging price table. Message price depends on sender, receiver or both and the variance is large.

Although our current coverage is limited to Spanish (es-ES) and English (en-UK), our architecture is designed to accommodate more languages and countries in the future, always adhering to ISO 639-1 and ISO 3166-1 standards.

### 4. Future Enhancements

As part of our long-term vision, we plan to extend support for:

- **Additional Languages:** We will continue to add more languages based on user feedback and regional demand, consistently using ISO 639-1 two-letter language codes.
- **Country Support:** Expand our country representation using ISO 3166-1 country codes.
- **Currency Support:** As we expand, we will introduce multi-currency support to cater to users from different financial regions.
- **Time Zones and Date Formats:** As we grow, we will continue to improve time zone handling and support additional date and time formats to accommodate users from various regions.

## Conclusion

Our commitment to internationalization follows the best practices and standards, ensuring that we can cater to users globally while providing them with an intuitive, adaptable experience. By leveraging ISO 639-1 for language codes, ISO 3166-1 for country codes, and other international standards, we create a robust, scalable localization approach.

The use of flags and phone numbers as conceptual aids helps users understand language and regional settings visually. Our approach to handling time zones and adhering to ISO 8601 ensures consistent date and time handling, while localizing the user experience when necessary. As we continue to grow, we will enhance our internationalization efforts, progressively implementing more standards to better serve our diverse user base.
